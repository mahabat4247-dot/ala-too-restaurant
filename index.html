Our story

A table set like a mountain feast

The name Ala-Too comes from the variegated peaks that rise across Kyrgyzstan, snow on top and green pasture below. For generations, families there have gathered around a shared dastarkhan: a spread of bread, sweets, tea and slow-cooked dishes that never seems to run out.

Our kitchen is built on that idea. We simmer meat and onions for hours, roll and pull noodles by hand, fry boorsok to order and keep the teapot full. Nobody should leave a Kyrgyz table hungry, or in a hurry.
